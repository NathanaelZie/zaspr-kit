# Code of conduct

[Version française](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fr.md)

ZASPR Kit is meant to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, harassment, threats or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive messages.

## Scope

This code applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, Nathanael ZIE ([@NathanaelZie](https://github.com/NathanaelZie)), or use GitHub's "Report content" feature on the message concerned. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior goes against this code.
