Theme Studio

Change the tokens of the JX theme and see a sample interface recompiled by the real engine, at the width of your choice. Copy the result into jx.yaml: only the tokens you changed are written, under theme.extend.

Colors

Each base color is shade 500; shades 100 to 900 are derived from it.

Typography

Font stacks use fonts installed on the system.

Spacing

One step of the p:, m:, gap: scale (1 to 16).

0.25rem
Radius

Scales br:sm to br:3xl together.

×1.0
Shadows

Strength of sh:sm to sh:2xl.

Borders

Widths of bw:1, bw:2 and bw:4, in px.

Breakpoints

Minimum widths of the responsive variants, in px.

# Same as the default theme: nothing to add to jx.yaml.

ZASPR Kit
